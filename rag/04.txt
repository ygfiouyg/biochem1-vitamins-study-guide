     Core characteristics
                                                                                                Two solubility groups
        Present in normal food in small concentrations.

                                                                                                        Water-soluble
        Do not enter major tissue structures.

                                                                                                         Fat-soluble
        Act in many body reactions as catalysts.


        Must be supplied by the diet                                                           Solubility influences storage,
                                                                                                 absorption and toxicity.




KEY POINT    Vitamins are classified mainly by solubility into water-soluble and fat-soluble groups.
